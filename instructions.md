# A Dark Room (Graphics)

## Documentation

- [A Dark Room](https://github.com/doublespeakgames/adarkroom/blob/main/README.md) — the original game's README.

## What you get on StartOS

- **Web UI**: the full game, played in your browser, with graphics: a fire that lights the room, a village you watch grow, a pixel-art world map and illustrated fights.
- **No account and no setup.** Anyone you give the address to can play; each person's game is their own.
- **Private play.** The game is served from your server and makes no outside requests while you play.

## Getting set up

1. Start the service.
2. Open the **Web UI**.
3. Light the fire.

## Using A Dark Room

### Graphics on and off

The game opens with graphics on. Click **graphics off.** in the menu at the bottom of the page to play the original text game, and **graphics on.** to switch back. Your game carries on either way.

On the world map, click anywhere above, below, left or right of the wanderer to walk that way (the arrow keys work too), and hover over a place to see its name.

### Your saved game

Your progress is saved in **your browser**, for the address you opened the game from. That means:

- Another browser, another computer, or another address for the same server (the LAN address, the Tor address or a custom domain) starts a new game.
- Clearing your browser's site data deletes your game.
- A StartOS backup does **not** include your game.

To keep a copy, or to move your game somewhere else, click **save.** in the game's menu and choose **export**; paste that text into **import** to load it back.

## Limitations

- Phones and tablets are sent to a page about the official mobile apps. Play the web version on a computer.
